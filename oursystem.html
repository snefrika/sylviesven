1. Opening 1♣

Shows a natural 1♣ opening or a balanced hand. The balanced hand is 11-14 or (17)18-19 HCP. It can contain a 5-card diamond suit.

Sequence 1♣-1♦♥

  • Transfer accept shows exact 3-card (bad 4333 possible).
  • Transfer accept with single jump (2♥ or 2♠) is 4-card support: 11-14 (semi)BAL or bad unbal hand.
  • Transfer accept with double jump (3♥ or 3♠) is unbal, not minimum but doesn't show much extras.
  • 2NT is (17)18-19 BAL, can still have 3-card support (or 4 spades).
  • 3NT is (17)18-19 BAL with 4-card support.
  • 1NT is 11-14 BAL OR 1(34)5, denies 3-card support, can still have 4 spades.
  • 1♠ shows 4 spades and always unbalanced, so 5+ clubs (or 4144).
  • 2♣/3♣ are standard but deny 3-card support.
  • Reverses are standard and show (15)16+.
  • Jump reverses:
    • 1♣-1♥, 3♦: Strong unbal raise, not GF.
    • 1♣-1♥, 3♥???
    • 1♣-1♦, 3♦: Strong unbal raise, not GF.

Sequence 1♣-1♠

Responder has denied a 4-card major unless GF, with diamonds. (If only INV with diamonds and a 4-card major, transfer to your major first.)

Opener rebids 1NT with 11-14 BAL. Rebids 2♣ with long clubs 11-15. Opener can reverse into a major, 16-19 HCP. A 2NT rebid is 18-19 as usual.

Opener's rebid of 2♦ is natural: 4 diamonds and 5+ clubs, but does not show extras: 11-15. If responder has not 4 diamonds he will have at least 5 clubs. With a 9-card club fit the three level is safe. (Also OPPS have at least a 9-card major fit — or 2×8-card major fits.) Responder passes or corrects to 3♣ when weak. 3♦: 2NT is INV, others are GF. (With (31)45, 16 ish, you can reverse in the three card.) After opener's 2♣ rebid, 2♦ is now F1. If responder rebids 2♦♠ it shows a GF hand with 5+♦ and 4 in the major.

1♣-1♠, 1NT

  • 2♣ — INV or weak diamonds
  • 2♦ — GF
  • 2♥♠ — Natural 4+ and diamonds, GF

The 1NT 11-12 BAL response

Opener passes or bids 2♣ with 11-12(13) hands. Other bids are natural and GF. Opener's 2NT shows a balanced hand with a 4-card major (or both). Opener's 2♦♥ rebid is natural unbalanced, GF. Jumps are shortness. If they intervene T/O X.

The 2♣ response (clubs, INV+)

Opener rebids 2NT (11-12 BAL) or 3♣ with minimum hands; others are GF.

1NT — Stayman (2♣)

Stayman shows at least one 4-card major (or longer), always! - Invitational hands with 5 spades: bid Stayman then 2♠. - 5-4 in majors: use Stayman. (But weak with 5 spades and 4 hearts: just transfer to spades.) - If only interested in game: use 3♣ Muppet stayman. - Transfers after transfers for the majors, but not after Stayman. (2NT invite is needed after Stayman.) - 4♣♦ = transfer to 4M, non-slammish (even after intervention if bid with jump). - 4♥♠ = to play, something to protect.

Responses to Stayman: 2♦, 2♥, 2♠.

Sequence 1NT-2♣, 2♦

  • Pass — weak, 5+ diamonds (and at least 34 in majors)
  • 2♥ — Weak with (4)5 hearts and 4 spades
  • 2♠ — INV with 5+ spades!
  • 2NT — INV, at least one 4-card major
  • 3♣♦ — Natural 5+, GF (no transfers after Stayman)
  • 3♥♠ — Smolen: shows 4 and 5+ in other major

Smolen: 1NT-2♣, 2♦-3♠ → 3♠ shows 4 spades and 5+ hearts. Smolen: 1NT-2♣, 2♦-3♥ → 3♥ shows 5+ spades and 4 hearts. (With 5-5 bid direct 3♦ over 1NT.)

Sequence 1NT-2♣, 2♥/2♠

  • 2♠ — INV with 5+ spades
  • 2NT — INV, at least one 4-card major. Over 2♥ this shows 4 spades.
  • 3♣♦ — Natural 5+, GF (and shows 4 in other major)
  • Raise — Raises are raises
  • 3OM — Fit, slammish
  • 3NT — Shows 4 cards in other major
  • 4♣♦ — Splinter

If opponents double 2♣

Redouble = suggestion to play. 2♦♥♠ are standard responses but show a club stopper. Without club stopper: Pass.

After Pass (sequence 1NT-(Pass)-2♣-(X), Pass): Responder bids 2♦ and XX with the weak Stayman hands (2♦ shows diamonds and the majors; XX is weak with both majors). 2♥ and 2♠ are natural and forcing! Lim+, 2♠ can be 5-card.

After XX (1NT-(Pass)-2♣-(X), XX-(Pass)):

  • Pass: some clubs and some points
  • 2♦: weak three suiter
  • 2♥: weak majors (not forcing!)
  • 2♠: natural and forcing INV+, can be 5-card
  • 2NT: INV with at least one major, so hearts in this case

1NT — Transfer to hearts (1NT-2♦)

Opener usually bids 2♥. Now 2♠ is invitational with 5+ hearts!

  • Pass — Weak with hearts
  • 2♠ — INV with 5+ hearts. If opener rebids 2NT (minimum), 3m is to play with 5♥-5m.
  • 2NT-3♣ — Transfer to a 4+ card minor
  • 3♦ — Transfer, 6+ hearts, INV+
  • 3♥ — Transfer, shows 5+ hearts and 4 spades, slammish
  • 3♠ — Transfer to 3NT, asks for fit.
  • 3NT — COG (choice of games)
  • 4♣♦ — Splinter, 6+ hearts, slammish
  • 4♥ — "I forgot to bid 4♣"

The 2♠ INV hand rebid (1NT-2♦, 2♥-2♠): Showing 5 hearts and invitational strength. Opener rebids 2NT with minimum and no heart fit. He can rebid 3♥/4♥ with fit and min/max. With max and no 3-card hearts opener can bid something else, usually 3NT. Over the 2NT rebid, responder can bid 3m to play 5-5. (And can rebid 3♥ with 5 hearts and 4 spades invitational.)

If OPPS double (1NT-(Pass)-2♦-(X))

Pass denies three card support. (Now XX is retransfer, 2♥ weak to play.) XX and 2♥ show support. 2♥ also shows diamond stopper. XX does not show playable contract but support!!!

1NT — Transfer to spades (1NT-2♥)

Never done with an invitational hand and only 5 spades — use Stayman with 5 spades and invitational hand.

  • Pass — Weak with spades
  • 2NT-3♣ — Transfer to 4+ card minor
  • 3♦ — Transfer, 4 hearts (and 5+ spades), slammish
  • 3♥ — Transfer, shows 6 spades, INV+
  • 3♠ — Transfer to 3NT, asks for fit.
  • 3NT — To play
  • 4♣♦ — Splinter, slammish
  • 4♥ — Splinter! Slammish. With 5-5 or longer bid 3♦.

Transfer over Transfer for a minor

E.g. 1NT-2♥, 2♠-3♣: shows 5 spades and 4+ diamonds. Still looking for the best game but could be slammish. Opener can bid 3M or on the four level with fit in the major. Opener can rebid 3OM with 5-card. Opener can rebid 3NT with double stoppers in both other suits (and no great fit for responder). Opener accepts the transfer with usually a fit, but could be a three card if he doesn't stop the other two suits.

Transfer over Transfer to other major

Shows a slammish 5+ – 4.

Transfer over Transfer to same major

Shows 6+ INV or slammish. (Just game forcing: bid direct 4M or 4m.)

1NT-2♠: Range ask / clubs

Either a hand with long clubs (weak or GF) or a balanced hand (either INV or slammish). - Opener bids 2NT if minimal, 3♣ when maximum. When in doubt look at the club holding. - If responder bids a suit on the three level: shows long clubs and shortage. (3♣ over 2NT is to play, weak.) - 4NT by responder asks for 4-card suits to play 6 in suit instead of NT.

Range ask got doubled (1NT-(Pass)-2♠-(X)): 2NT and 3♣ additionally show a stopper in spades. XX suggests a playable contract. Pass denies a spade stopper, over which partner can redouble to reask, or bid 3♣ (to play) or higher as before.

1NT-2NT: Transfer to diamonds or both minors

Either weak or GF. If diamonds and GF, always with shortness (or very slammish — you will need to avoid 4♦). If with both minors you cannot stop in 3NT!

Opener rebids his better minor. 1NT-2NT, 3m, and now responder can:

  • Pass
  • 3♦ — Weak with diamonds
  • 3♥♠ — Shortness and diamonds

Sequence with both minors and GF: 1NT-2NT, 3m-4m. Now 4NT is sign off.

Sequence 1NT-2NT, 3♦-3NT: Responder is showing 3361-ish. As opener diamonds are better than clubs, opener will rarely pass.

After responder has shown single: If opener now bids 4M (not in single) it is natural showing a 5-card.

4m support shows 5-5 forcing (4♦ could be with diamonds). 4♦ over 3♣: Strong with diamonds.

1NT-3♣: Puppet stayman

  • 3♦ — At least one 4-card major
  • 3♥ — 5-cqrd hearts
  • 3♠ — 5-card spades
  • 3NT — No -card major.

  • Opener bids 4♥♠ without much slam interest
  • Opener bids 4♣♦ with a very good hand for ♥♠ (next step is retransfer, not Last Train)

3. Opening 2♣

This is either a weak 2♦ opening, or a strong hand. The strong hands are 4-5 losers, GF hands: two-suiters with 5-card major or a strong NT hand: 22-23 or 26-27.

Responses:

  • 2♦ — Most hands bid 2♦
  • 2♥♠ — INV vs weak 2♦, 5+ card. Not forcing!
  • 2NT — INV+ relay
  • 3♦ — PRE, but prepared for the strong hands

Over the 2♦ waiting bid

  • 2♥ — Hearts and another GF, or NT 26-27, forces 2♠, and now:
    • 2NT — 26-27
    • 3♣ — Natural 5 hearts and (4)5+ clubs. GF
    • 3♦ — Natural 5 hearts and (4)5+ diamonds. GF
    • 3♥ — 6+ hearts, bad suit? With 6♥ GF open 2♦.
    • 3♠ — 5+ hearts, 4+ spades GF
    • 3NT — ?
    • 4♣♦ — ?6♥4m, not 5-5 (rebid 3m)
  • 2♠ — 5+ card, 3(4) losers. 2NT asks for 2nd suit.
  • 2NT — 22-23 BAL
  • 3♣♦ — Natural, 6+ card
  • 3♥♠ — 6-card, 4(5) losers
  • 3NT — ?
  • 4♣♦ — 3-card in 5530, 3 losers

Over the 2NT relay

Opener is weak; if not we play 6 or 7.

6. Conventions

XYZ (Double Deux)

General principles after a 1-1-1 sequence:

  • All INV hands go over 2♣
  • 2♦ is artificial GF
  • 2NT is transfer to 3♣
  • Other bids are weak or GF

When XYZ applies

  1. Opener has opened on the one level in 1st or 2nd hand. (No XYZ with passed partner.)
  2. Responder has bid or doubled.
  3. Opener rebids on the one level, or does a support double over a one-level overcall. (Also with 1NT rebid, and RHO passes after opener's rebid.)
